Teaching

Composition, computer music, programming for artists, music perception, and performance philosophy, taught at the Hochschule für Musik und Theater Hamburg, Northeastern University, UC Berkeley, the California College of the Arts, and McGill University; and workshops in Max, odot, and Interstitial Listening in Europe and North America. The approach is the one the works take: construct and care for the context, and make space for people to enter it on their own terms.

Courses

Hochschule für Musik und Theater Hamburg 2019–2022

Lecturer

  • Kinetics in Sound and Space: Wavefield Synthesis. The technical and æsthetic dimensions of composition for a wavefield array.
  • Kinetics in Sound and Space: C Programming for Media/Arts Applications. Introduction to C programming in the context of authoring Max/MSP externals.

Interstitial Listening

A multi-sensory listening practice co-developed with Teoma Naccarato since 2016. It tunes awareness to the processes by which rhythms emerge in movement and music, and is a technique for exploring and reimagining habits of relation between humans and machines. At its center is a simple but impossible task: to synchronize with an unpredictable metronome.

The Provocations Project

A series of open calls for provocations, to spur dialogue about differences that matter within and between cultures of research in the arts, humanities, and sciences. Founded with Teoma Naccarato, and coordinated with Jessica Rajko. Once launched, each call stays open, and contributions are published online as they arrive, alongside curated video conversations between contributors, roundtables, and hybrid events. MacCallum co-chaired the Provocations track of SloMoCo 2021, a year-long, hybrid conference on movement and computing.
