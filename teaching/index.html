Teaching

My approach to teaching is the same as my approach to performance: construct and care for the context, and make space for people to enter it on their own terms. I understand teaching as mentorship, and mentorship as a balance of expertise and restraint.

Courses

Hochschule für Musik und Theater Hamburg 2019–2022

Lecturer

  • Kinetics in Sound and Space: Wavefield Synthesis. The technical and æsthetic dimensions of composition for a wavefield array.
  • Kinetics in Sound and Space: C Programming for Media/Arts Applications. Introduction to C programming in the context of authoring Max/MSP externals.
  • Programming Languages for Multimedia Composition and Performance. Survey of programming languages used in multimedia: Max/MSP, Pure Data, SuperCollider, OM#, Isadora, TouchDesigner, C, C++, assembly, Lisp, Python, Haskell, JavaScript, Java.
  • Science of Music. Introduction to music perception and cognition.
  • Performance Philosophy: Artistic Practice in Times of Social Unrest. A survey of performance philosophy, addressing performance itself as philosophy, and philosophy as performance. Discussions included Black Lives Matter, #MeToo, climate change, and political unrest. Co-taught with Teoma Naccarato.
  • Performance Philosophy: On the Critical Appropriation of Technology in Artistic Practice. Appropriation, transgression, mastery, and misuse of technologies across disciplines. Co-taught with Teoma Naccarato.

California College of the Arts 2012–2013

Lecturer

  • Math and Media. Artistic practice and mathematical concepts such as measure, space, and abstraction.

Northeastern University, College of Arts, Media and Design 2011–2012

Visiting Professor

  • Music Composition Seminar. Undergraduate seminar on spectral harmony, randomness, open-form notation, and extended techniques.
  • Composition Lessons. Weekly individual lessons for eleven composition students.
  • Composition with Random Processes. The technical, historical, and æsthetic aspects of randomness in music.
  • Interactive Real-Time Performance. Max/MSP, with an emphasis on sound synthesis, composition, and real-time human-computer interaction.
  • Music Since 1900. Musical styles and concepts in Europe and North America in the twentieth century.

UC Berkeley, Department of Music 2005–2011

Lecturer, 2010–2011; Graduate Student Instructor, 2005–2007

  • Music Composition. Undergraduate seminar on spectral harmony, randomness, open-form notation, extended techniques, and musique concrète.
  • Introduction to Computer Music. Programming in Max/MSP, computer music, and digital audio (with Edmund Campion).
  • Music Perception and Cognition. Auditory and cerebral physiology, auditory illusions, and auditory scene analysis.
  • Introduction to Western Music. Sonata form, early twentieth-century music, and the music and politics of post-war Darmstadt.

McGill University, Faculty of Music 2000–2002

Teaching Assistant

  • Melody and Counterpoint, Renaissance Style. Species counterpoint.
  • Elementary Harmony and Analysis. Four-part harmony, diatonic chords, tonality, and modulation.
