--- authors: "J MacCallum, A Freed, D Wessel" title: "New tools for aspect-oriented programming in music and media programming environments" publication: "Proceedings of the International Computer Music Conference" volume: "Athens, Greece" pages: "1587-1591" year: "2014" file: "/assets/papers/2014_MacCallumFreedWessel_ICMC_AspectOrientedProgramming.pdf" abstract: "Media/arts programming is often experimental and exploratory in nature and requiring a flexible development environment to enable continually changing requirements and to facilitate iterative design in which the development of software impacts the design of a work of art, which in turn produces new requirements for the software. We discuss agile development as it relates to media/arts programming. We present aspect-oriented programming and its implementation in Max/MSP using Open Sound Control and the odot library as tool for mobilizing the benefits of agile development." ptags: map layout: publication ---
