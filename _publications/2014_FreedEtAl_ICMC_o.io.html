--- authors: "A Freed, D DeFilippo, R Gottfried, J MacCallum, J Lubow, D Razo, D Wessel" title: "o.io: A unified communications framework for music, intermedia, and cloud interaction" publication: "Proceedings of the International Computer Music Conference" volume: "Athens, Greece" pages: "1592-1597" year: "2014" file: "/assets/papers/2014_FreedEtAl_ICMC_o.io.pdf" abstract: 'We present work on the "o.io" system, a suite of tools for hiding vendor-specific and protocolspecific details of controllers and actuators and for replacing diffuse documentation and heterogeneous ontologies with harmonized, situational schema carried along in real-time with gesture and actuator control values as Open Sound Control (OSC) bundles. We introduce useful general design patterns and object-oriented tools that support them. We conclude with details of support of two particular devices that illustrate the potential of "o.io", the QuNeo and Bluetooth LE heart rate monitors.' ptags: map layout: publication ---
